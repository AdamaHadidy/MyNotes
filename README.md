# MyNotes

A notes and PDF annotation app that runs on **iPad (or any browser)**, **Mac** and **Windows**.
Write with Apple Pencil, a pen tablet, a trackpad or a mouse. Everything is stored on your own device. There is no account and no server.

|---|---|
| **Use it in a browser or on iPad** | https://adamahadidy.github.io/MyNotes/ |
| **Download for Mac and Windows** | https://github.com/adamahadidy/MyNotes/releases/latest |

## What it does

**Writing and drawing**
- Pen, ballpoint, fountain pen and pencil, plus a highlighter and two eraser types (whole stroke or pixel)
- One-tap pen presets and a color wheel with recent colors
- Hold at the end of a stroke to snap it into a clean line, circle, rectangle or triangle (switch on or off)
- Ruler for straight lines, and a laser pointer for presenting
- Lasso: circle ink, text and photos to move, resize, copy, recolor or delete them
- Text boxes with 19 fonts (including handwriting and Arabic fonts), sizes, colors, bold, italic, underline, alignment and word highlights
- Photos and stickers, and a scientific calculator you can drop results from onto the page

**PDFs**
- Annotate any PDF, and export it with your ink as sharp vector lines
- Highlight, underline and strike out real PDF text (snaps to the lines of text)
- Insert blank pages, delete, reorder, bookmark, and use the PDF's own table of contents
- Page thumbnails, vertical scrolling or one or two pages at a time
- Search for words inside PDFs and notebooks, from Home or inside a document
- Open a second document beside the one you are writing in

**Notebooks and organizing**
- Paper types: blank, lined, grid, dots, Cornell notes, weekly planner, to-do list and graph axes
- Notebook covers, folders (inside folders too), pins, drag and drop, multi-select
- Recently Deleted (kept for 30 days), a sidebar with recent notes, light and dark themes and color schemes

**Export and safety**
- Export all pages, one page or a range, as a PDF, a flattened PDF, or pictures (a `.zip` for several pages)
- Back up everything to one file and restore it any time
- Storage meter, save warnings and backup reminders

## Install it

### On iPad or iPhone
1. Open the web address above in **Safari**. Stay online for about ten seconds the first time.
2. Tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Open MyNotes from the Home Screen. It now works offline.

### On a Mac
1. Go to the [latest release](https://github.com/adamahadidy/mynotes/releases/latest) and download `MyNotes-…-mac.dmg`.
2. Open the file and drag **MyNotes** into **Applications**.
3. The first time, **right-click** the app, choose **Open**, then **Open** again.

Works on Intel and Apple Silicon Macs.

### On Windows
1. Go to the [latest release](https://github.com/adamahadidy/mynotes/releases/latest) and download `MyNotes-Setup-….exe`.
2. Run it. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. MyNotes opens and adds a Start menu shortcut.

## Your notes and privacy

- Notes, PDFs and photos are stored **only on your device**, inside the app's own storage. Nothing is uploaded.
- The iPad and browser version, the Mac app and the Windows app each have **separate storage**. They do not sync with each other.
- Handwriting fonts and the PDF tools load from the internet the first time. After that the app works offline. The Mac and Windows apps include the PDF tools, so they work offline from the first launch.
- Where the desktop data lives: `~/Library/Application Support/MyNotes` on Mac and `%APPDATA%\MyNotes` on Windows.

### Moving notes between devices (backup and restore)
1. On the old device: **Home**, then the **⋯** button, then **Back up everything**. Save the `.json` file.
2. Send that file to the new device (AirDrop, email, Files, a USB drive).
3. On the new device: **Home**, then **⋯**, then **Restore from a backup**, and pick the file.

Back up before you clear Safari data, reset a device, or move to a new address. MyNotes reminds you every couple of weeks.
